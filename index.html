My beautiful Julie,

But I can promise to keep learning how to love you better, to respect you, to be honest with you, and to cherish the little moments we build together. If you will keep walking beside me, I want to keep making this story ours—one sweet memory at a time.

Always yours, with a grateful heart, your boy ♡
